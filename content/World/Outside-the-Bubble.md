# 🌿 Outside the Bubble — Points of Interest

The outside of the bubble is open and walkable, surrounded by dense alien trees and shrubs. It feels expansive but contained — a stark contrast to the controlled environment inside the settlement.

All five areas are accessible in full once the player's first outside cutscene plays. **No progressive gates.** [[Farming/Himmies|Himmies]] are spread across the entire landscape — not tied to any one area.

> For most of the settlement's history, **no human has ventured out here.** The player is the first.

---

## 1. Exterior Farm

The player's **second farm** — where all Raea crop farming happens. Crystal nodes, pod nodes, alien ecosystem objects. Contrasts sharply with the controlled interior farm inside the bubble.

**Narrative significance:** The player planting roots in the world Mayor Johnson said was off-limits. The exterior farm growing and thriving is the **visual proof** that sustainable farming outside the bubble is possible — the core argument of the trade agreement at the end of Chapter 2.

See [[Farming/index|Farming Overview]] for how the system works.

---

## 2. Tanilian Village

A small settlement of huts. Home to [[Characters/Tanilians/Elaren|Elaren]], [[Characters/Tanilians/Daven|Daven]], [[Characters/Tanilians/Sevani|Sevani]], [[Characters/Tanilians/Linera|Linera]], [[Characters/Tanilians/Reni|Reni]], [[Characters/Tanilians/Mevi|Mevi]], and background NPCs.

This is where the player builds relationships with the Tanilians, learns Raea farming alongside them, and where the trade agreement arc plays out.

The **empty food box** that Atkins used to fill lives here — the first thing [[Characters/Tanilians/Reni|Reni]] leads the player to when she approaches asking for food.

---

## 3. Mine Entrance

Source of minerals for crystal node maintenance and crystal crop seeds/materials.

## 4. Foraging Area

Dense with native Raea flora. This is where the player finds:
- Wild plants
- Pod node materials
- Root crop seeds
- Foraged materials for the [[Farming/Himmies#the-mushroom-guy|Mushroom Guy]] summoning item

---

## 5. Fishing Spot

Quiet and atmospheric. Likely [[Characters/Alex|Alex]]'s domain given his love of fishing and the role it plays in his character arc ("always keep your line in the water").
