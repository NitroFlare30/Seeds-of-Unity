# 🚀 Ships & Arrivals

Three colony ships traveled from Earth to Planet Raea. Each arrival shaped the settlement's history. Two additional ships were expected after Solace but **never arrived** — their fate is unknown.

---

## The Three Ships

| Ship | Arrival | Crew | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Serenity** | ~2072 (first) | Matthew Atkins, Catia Ziegler, Jaycee Willow, Lubanzi Nkosi | All original crew deceased by game start |
| **Tranquility** | ~2073 (second) | Marco Everett, Damien Garcia, Charlotte Wilson, Miku Ishikawa | Charlotte Wilson is the only surviving original crew member |
| **Solace** | ~2134+ (most recent) | Jet Martin, Lucas Walker, Nikki Taylor, The Player | Arrived ~62 years after Serenity/Tranquility — first new arrivals in over six decades |

---

## Serenity (~2072)

The founding ship. **Matthew Atkins** served as the first mayor and led the original settlement. The Serenity crew established the bubble, the first farms, and first contact with the Tanilians (which went disastrously — see [[World/Settlement-History#the-atkins-incident|The Atkins Incident]]).

- **Matthew Atkins** — First mayor; charismatic but anxiety-driven; best friends with Lucas Walker on Earth; died of natural causes after the incident; grave is inside the bubble; had a secret affair with Jaycee Willow
- **Catia Ziegler** — Matriarch; her descendants (Kane, Miya, Celia, Eva) are some of the most prominent families in the settlement; also surrogate mother to Wilhelm and Amber
- **Jaycee Willow** — [[Characters/Alex|Alex]]'s mother; married Marco Everett; had an affair with Atkins (Darryl is Atkins' secret biological son); died connected to the Atkins Incident
- **Lubanzi Nkosi** — Miner and explorer; a cave in the settlement is named after him; [[Characters/Arno-Nkosi|Arno]]'s grandfather

---

## Tranquility (~2073)

- **Marco Everett** — Carpenter and builder of much of the settlement's infrastructure; [[Characters/Alex|Alex]]'s father; deceased
- **Damien Garcia** — Deceased; patriarch of the Garcia family
- **Charlotte Wilson** — The only surviving original crew member at game start; elderly, senile; cared for by [[Characters/Miya-Ziegler|Miya]]
- **Miku Ishikawa** — Deceased; died at or shortly after giving birth to her surrogate son Ben; her surrogate line includes [[Characters/Bella-Johnson|Bella]], [[Characters/Emily-Scott|Emily]], [[Characters/Ayla-White|Ayla]], [[Characters/Jesse-Wilson|Jesse]], and [[Characters/Bryan-Carrick|Bryan]]

---

## Solace (~2134+)

Arrived approximately **62 years after Serenity and Tranquility** — far later than expected. The player character was in cryosleep for that entire duration, waking to find everyone they expected to meet is long dead.

**[[Characters/Lucas-Walker|Lucas Walker]]** had been designated as Atkins' vice mayor and best friend on Earth. He arrived expecting a reunion and found a grave instead. He carries quiet guilt that he wasn't there when Atkins needed him.

---

## The Lost Ships

Two additional ships were expected after Solace. They **never arrived**. Their fate is unknown. The settlement holds onto hope but accepts they may be gone. Hardware that Nikki was brought to install was likely aboard one of these ships. Considered a potential DLC hook.
