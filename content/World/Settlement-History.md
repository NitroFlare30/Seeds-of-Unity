# 🏛️ Settlement History & Character Families

---

## The Atkins Incident

[[Characters/Alfred-Johnson|Mayor Johnson]]'s isolationist stance — and the fear that pervades the entire settlement — traces back to a single catastrophic event.

**Matthew Atkins** (Serenity, original crew) attempted to help the Tanilians by growing crops for them directly. He planted monoculture without understanding Raea's ecosystem, which caused root rot and massive crop failure. The failure triggered a **Mushroom Guy intervention** that caused casualties among the settlers.

### Casualties
- **Jaycee Willow** — Died in connection with the incident
- **Darryl ("Uncle Dink")** — Survived but was mentally broken by what he witnessed. He wandered outside the bubble and was presumed dead. He is still alive near the Tanilian village, running a junk shop, talking to the binary stars. Himmies keep him company. He has no memory of his past.
- **Matthew Atkins** — Survived the incident but died later of natural causes. **His grave is inside the bubble.**

### Consequences
- The whole settlement knows what happened; fear of going outside persists beyond Mayor Johnson's rule
- Soldiers were stationed specifically to prevent a repeat
- Mayor Johnson hardened his isolationist stance from **real grief** over real people lost — not abstract pragmatism
- [[Farming/Himmies|Himmies]] disappeared during the failure — the ecosystem had given up trying to warn Atkins

### Atkins' Secret
Atkins had an affair with **Jaycee Willow** while she was married to Marco Everett. **Darryl is Atkins' biological son**, not Marco's. Only Atkins and Jaycee knew this. Both are now dead. Darryl cannot access his memories. The secret is effectively buried.

## Lucas & Atkins

[[Characters/Lucas-Walker|Lucas Walker]] was designated as Atkins' vice mayor and right-hand on Earth. They were best friends during training before the missions. Lucas arrived on the Solace 62 years after Atkins, expecting to reunite with him, and found him long dead. **Lucas carries quiet guilt that he wasn't there when Atkins needed him.**

Two additional ships were expected after Solace but never arrived. Their fate is unknown.
