# 🌍 Planet Raea

Planet Raea is humanity's first interstellar colony, home to an enclosed bubble settlement founded in ~2072. The planet orbits a **binary star system** — one smaller red star and one larger blue star — which drives its unique five-season cycle and alien ecosystem.

> **Current year:** ~2134+ (Solace arrival year)
> **Settlement founded:** ~2072 (Serenity arrival)
> **Bubble restriction:** Settlement residents are prohibited from leaving the bubble by Mayor's decree

---

## The Bubble Settlement

The settlement is enclosed in a protective bubble — not merely a symbol of control, but **necessary infrastructure**: Earth crops cannot survive Raea's raw binary star radiation and alien ecosystem without it. The bubble allows familiar agriculture inside while keeping the alien world at bay.

The outside of the bubble is walkable and expansive, surrounded by dense alien trees and shrubs. For most of the settlement's history, no human has ventured out.

## Binary Star System

Raea orbits two stars:
- **Blue star** — larger, dominant; drives Teal Season (high UV, crystal crop growth)
- **Red star** — smaller; drives Plum Season (warm infrared, flowering crop growth)

Their relative positions create **five distinct seasons** rather than Earth's four. See [[Farming/Five-Seasons]] for the full breakdown.
