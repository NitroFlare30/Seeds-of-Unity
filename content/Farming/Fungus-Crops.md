# 🍄 Fungus Crops

Fungal crops are **bioluminescent, eerie, and mysterious**. They thrive **only during Gray Season** and provide powerful stat boosts to the player — making them some of the most valuable crops on Raea.

All fungal crops are **Gray Season exclusive**. Unlock method: **Gray Season arrival** (naturally available during Eclipse).

Primary use is as **ingredients** — likely for power-ups, consumables, and possibly food. Each crop's stat boost is delivered through whatever form that crafting takes (potions, meals, or otherwise — TBD).

## Gray Season & Availability

All four crops are **exclusively available during Gray Season**:
- **Impossible to grow** in other seasons
- **Seeds appear naturally** as Gray Season begins (no unlock quest required)
- **Rarest crops** — encourage exploration and experimentation during the Eclipse
- **Powerful incentive** to farm during the uncomfortable, eerie Gray Season

While not explicitly taught by Tanilians, fungal crops resonate with Tanilian themes:

- Tanilians may recognize these as native Raean lifeforms that thrive in darkness
