# 🌸 Flower Crops

Flowering crops are colorful, seasonal, and visually distinctive. They serve a mix of food, decorative, and ecosystem purposes — the most varied category of Raea crops.

Best season: **[[Five-Seasons#Plum Season|Plum Season]]**
Unlock method: Quests, [[Himmies|Himmie]] reveals, [[Crystal-Nodes|Pod node]] surprises.

---

## The Five Flower Crops

### Gilvara
| | |
|---|---|
| **Shape** | Large bulb that blooms open at maturity; fruit clusters hang inside |
| **Color** | Iridescent pink-gold |
| **Flavor** | Sweet — pomegranate/blueberry-like |
| **Uses** | Food |
| **Notes** | The bulb stays closed during growth and opens when ready to harvest, making ripeness visually obvious. The fruit clusters hang from the inner petals like jewels. Striking under Plum season's red-tinted light. |

---

### Sorveth
| | |
|---|---|
| **Shape** | Holds seed pods while growing; matures into a full, rose-like bloom |
| **Color** | Orange |
| **Flavor** | Not edible |
| **Uses** | Decorative; passive ecosystem restoration |
| **Notes** | Harvesting the flower releases small pollinator creatures that fly to nearby water and seed coral, passively replenishing the underwater ecosystem. No player intervention required after harvest. Players who grow this regularly may notice healthier water areas over time. |

---

### Thyrvane
| | |
|---|---|
| **Shape** | Flowering plant above ground; yam-shaped crop underground |
| **Color** | Soft lavender flowers; dusty rose yam |
| **Flavor** | Savory — light, chicken-like |
| **Uses** | Food |
| **Notes** | A dual-layer plant — the flowers are visible above ground while the actual harvest is below. Lighter and more delicate than the [[Root-Crops#Quorrith|Quorrith]] meat analog. The lavender blooms make it one of the prettier crops on the farm. |

---

### Raean Salt
| | |
|---|---|
| **Shape** | Red pods clustered on a flowering stem |
| **Color** | Deep red |
| **Flavor** | Grainy, seasoned salt — spice-like |
| **Uses** | Food (flavor enhancement) |
| **Notes** | Not a main ingredient — used to season and enhance other dishes. Fills the spice role in recipes. The red color intuitively signals its use to the player. |

---

### Blushpetal
| | |
|---|---|
| **Shape** | Soft, full bloom |
| **Color** | Pink |
| **Flavor** | Not edible |
| **Uses** | Animal breeding trigger |
| **Notes** | Used on animals to trigger breeding. Single-purpose crop — gives players a dedicated reason to keep some growing at all times. Raea's equivalent of the Miracle Potion. |
