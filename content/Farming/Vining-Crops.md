# Vining Crops

**Vining crops** are a distinctive crop family that grow laterally across the ground, connecting adjacent plants to form farming nodes. Their individual harvest yield is low — the real payoff comes from the node they create.

Both vining subtypes are unlocked through quests and Himmie reveals rather than foraging or mining.

---

## How Vining Crops Work

When a vining crop grows adjacent to a compatible crop, it bridges the two and forms a **node** between them. The node is the primary output of the vine — the vine itself produces little on its own.

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| **Direct harvest yield** | Low |
| **Primary output** | The node formed between connected crops |
| **Season sensitivity** | Yes — node output is influenced by the current season |
| **Unlock method** | Quests, [[Himmies\|Himmie]] reveals |

---

## Crystal Vines

**Connect crystal crops → form Crystal Nodes**

Crystal vines grow between [[Crop-Families\|crystal / mineral crops]] and bridge them into a **[[Crystal-Nodes\|Crystal Node]]**. Crystal nodes provide irrigation and channel Raea's mineral resources to nearby crops.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Compatible crops** | Crystal / mineral crops |
| **Node formed** | [[Crystal-Nodes\|Crystal Node]] |
| **Node function** | Irrigation — enhances crop growth |
| **Maintenance** | Minerals (from mining) |

---

## Regular Vines

**Connect other crops → form Pod Nodes**

Regular vines grow between non-crystal crops and form a **[[Crystal-Nodes\|Pod Node]]**. Pod nodes produce random crop output when mature, introducing variety without explicit player knowledge.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Compatible crops** | Root, Flowering, Fungal crops |
| **Node formed** | [[Crystal-Nodes\|Pod Node]] |
| **Node function** | Random crop output — influenced by season |
| **Maintenance** | Foraged materials |

---

## Season Effects

All vining crops and their nodes are season-sensitive. Pod node output in particular skews toward crops that thrive in the current season — making vine placement timing a meaningful strategic choice. See [[Five-Seasons]] for seasonal context.
