# 🌾 Raea Farming System

Raea crops are completely separate from Earth crops due to binary star UV radiation. The farming system uses **the same core mechanics** as Earth farming (watering, fertilizer, harvest) but feels alien through phased growth, seasonal cycles, crystal nodes, and ecosystem feedback via [[Himmies|Himmies]].

---

## Quick Reference

| System | Details |
|---|---|
| **Total crops** | ~30 by end-game |
| **Crop families** | 5 (Root, Flowering, Fungal, Crystal, Vining) |
| **Starting crops** | 2–3 (1 root, 1 flowering) |
| **Seasons** | 5 (Teal, Purple Descent, Gray, Violet Emergence, Plum) |
| **Key mechanic** | Crystal nodes need mineral maintenance |
| **Feedback system** | Himmies indicate ecosystem health and warn of problems |

---

## Core Mechanics (Same as Earth)

- **Watering** — identical to Earth crops
- **Fertilizer** — identical to Earth crops
- **Harvest** — identical to Earth crops (produces food)
- **Growth timers** — 4–12 days or full season
- **Soil fertility** — coming later

---

## What Makes Raea Crops Different

1. **Phased growth** instead of linear progression
2. **[[Crystal-Nodes|Crystal nodes & ecosystem objects]]** required for growth
3. **[[Five-Seasons|Binary star seasonal system]]** — 5 distinct seasons, not Earth seasons
4. **[[Himmies]]** — visual ecosystem feedback system

---

## The Two Farms

| Farm | Location | Crops |
|---|---|---|
| **Interior farm** | Inside the bubble | Earth crops only |
| **[[../World/Outside-the-Bubble\|Exterior farm]]** | Outside the bubble | Raea crops only |

The exterior farm is where the player's Raea farming happens — and its success is the **visual proof** of sustainable practice that drives the Chapter 2 trade agreement.
