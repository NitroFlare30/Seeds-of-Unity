# 🌸 Himmies

Himmies are small, circular native creatures of Raea with a large flower on their head. They are **sentient but not intelligent** — think "mute spirits." Symbiotic with the Raea ecosystem. They serve as the player's primary feedback system for farm and ecosystem health.

- **Only the player** can meaningfully interact with Himmies
- Foreign and unknown to all human settlers until the player arrives
- Spread across the **entire outside landscape** — not tied to one specific spot
- Player learns what they mean **organically through observation** — no tutorial
- [[../Characters/Tanilians/index|Tanilians]] also observe Himmies and learn alongside the player — shared discovery

---

## Presence Mechanic

Himmies show up **when the ecosystem has something to say** — good or bad. Absence doesn't mean things are fine; it means nothing is happening worth communicating. As the player engages more with their farm, Himmies appear more frequently.

> **Historical note:** When Mayor Atkins planted monoculture crops, the Himmies didn't disappear because the farm was dying. **They disappeared because the ecosystem had given up trying to warn him.** There was nothing left to say.

## Himmie Types

### Health / Corruption Spectrum

| Type | Description |
|---|---|
| **Elated** | Peak ecosystem health. Warm, vibrant colors. Fully bloomed flower. May float or glow. *Visuals TBD.* |
| **Happy** | Thriving ecosystem. Warmer/brighter colors, bigger bloom, more energetic than Content. |
| **Content** | Baseline healthy. Calm, flower fully bloomed. Sleeps peacefully next to thriving crops. |
| **Corrupting** | Early-stage corruption. Flower wilting, appearance darkening. **Warning stage** — course correct now. Triggered by monoculture, poor soil, wrong season. |
| **Johimmy** | Fully corrupted. **Hostile to the player.** Squirts something from its flower that drains stamina. Cursed appearance. |
| **Dead** | Gone. Fix the ecosystem and they resurrect on their own — no karma meter, no extra mechanic. |

### Seasonal Type

| Type | Description |
|---|---|
| **Transition Himmie** | Appears when a season is about to change. Flower slowly shifts toward the incoming season's palette. One type covers all five seasons — player reads the flower color. |

**Final roster: 7 types** — Elated, Happy, Content, Corrupting, Johimmy, Dead, Transition

## The Mushroom Guy

An unnamed, unexplained entity that appears on the farm periodically. No name. Never speaks. No official lore explanation — **intentionally mysterious**. Players will name him themselves.

**What he does:**
- Appears to be randomly bullying Himmies — shoving them, punching them, walking away
- Actually performing **brutal ecosystem maintenance**: checking and correcting corrupted Himmies before they become Johimmies
- Early appearances seem chaotic; later the player understands he's a necessary if violent force

**First encounter — automatic cutscene:**
- Triggered when the player creates their first **Johimmy** through monoculture (because they didn't know better)
- The Johimmy attacks the player and drains stamina
- The Mushroom Guy appears and destroys the Johimmy with no explanation, then walks away
- Teaches the monoculture lesson without punishing the player too hard — he saves them this time

**After first encounter:**
- Player must **summon him** to deal with future Johimmies
- Summoned via a craftable item made from foraged materials — specifics TBD
