# Kane Ziegler

> *"I don't have time for this. Or you, frankly."*

**Generation:** Gen 2 | **Ship:** Native | **Archetype:** Anti-Hero | **Romanceable:** Yes

---

## Personality & Voice

Dismissive, arrogant, intellectual snobbery — Kane is genuinely brilliant and has decided that's a good reason to treat everyone else as beneath him.

**Speech patterns:** Insults about intelligence disguised as casual observations. Short dismissive responses. Condescending tone. Refuses small talk. Extremely impatient.

**Emotions used:** Neutral (primarily)

---

## Key Traits

- Extremely busy and impatient — time is his most valued resource
- Acts superior to everyone; believes it
- Insulting about the player's intelligence, casually
- Doesn't respect farming work at all
- Hardworking — the arrogance is earned from results, not nothing
- Refuses to show kindness; dry humor surfaces occasionally
- **[[Miya-Ziegler|Miya]]'s brother** — they share a last name but not a temperament

## Heart Arc

**Early:** Rude and dismissive. Tells the player to leave him alone and means it.
**Mid:** Still arrogant, but warmed only through persistent effort. Grudging acknowledgment.
**High:** Maintains arrogance but a dry, genuine respect emerges. Reveals he respects dedication — and shows real humor that isn't a weapon.
