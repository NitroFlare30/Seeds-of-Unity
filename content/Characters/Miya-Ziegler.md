# Miya Ziegler

> *"Have you eaten today? Don't lie to me. I can tell."*

**Generation:** Gen 2 | **Ship:** Native | **Archetype:** Supporting | **Romanceable:** Yes

---

## Personality & Voice

Caring, responsible, hardworking, motherly — Miya is the person who keeps everyone together, even when she's falling apart herself.

**Speech patterns:** Warm and concerned. Mentions her responsibilities. Uses humor to soften authority ("angry Miya is not a fun Miya"). References the people she's caring for. Checks on everyone.

**Emotions used:** Happy, XD, Neutral, Annoyed

---

## Key Traits

- Caretaker and housekeeper for the settlement
- Works at the **clinic**
- Takes care of **Charlotte Wilson** (the elderly original Tranquility crew member)
- Worries about her brother [[Kane-Ziegler|Kane]] (works too much)
- Also cleans at the Zieglers' (big house) — handles the family legacy
- Ensures people take care of themselves, sometimes by force of personality
- **Even Mayor Johnson respects/fears her when she's angry** — she's the one person he defers to

## Heart Arc

**Early:** Immediately concerned about the player. Are they eating? Sleeping? She's watching.
**Mid:** Mother figure energy — warm, authoritative, checking in.
**High:** Reveals her own struggles and dreams. The caretaking isn't endless energy — it costs her. She wants someone to take care of her too.
