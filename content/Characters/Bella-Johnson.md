# Bella Johnson

> *"Ugh. Farming. My nails are already ruined."*

**Generation:** Gen 2 | **Ship:** Native | **Archetype:** Anti-Hero | **Romanceable:** Yes

---

## Personality & Voice

Sarcastic, spoiled, dramatic, condescending — and genuinely witty underneath all of it.

**Speech patterns:** Annoyed tone as default. Complains constantly. References her daddy and material goods. Dramatic sighs. Dismissive of anyone she deems beneath her. Vain — checks her makeup.

**Emotions used:** Annoyed (primary), Neutral, Happy (rarely)

---

## Key Traits

- Daddy's girl — [[Alfred-Johnson|Mayor Johnson]] gives her everything and she knows it
- Vain — checks her makeup constantly, keeps up appearances
- Rich and self-centered by settlement standards
- Bored with Raea and farming, treats it as beneath her
- Treats others as servants initially
- Can be genuinely witty and cutting — the sarcasm has real intelligence behind it

## Family Notes

Bella was born as one of **Ariana's surrogate children** and was adopted by **Mayor Johnson**, who never married and raised her alone. He genuinely loves her. She is, in every way that matters, his daughter.

Bella's birth mother Ariana is a cook at the inn — Bella knows about her to some degree, but the dynamic is TBD.

## Heart Arc

**Early:** Entitled, dismissive, treats the player as a nuisance.
**Mid:** Grudging acknowledgment that the player is still around. Slight softening. Still sarcastic.
**High:** Drops the armor. The vain exterior is protection. Reveals vulnerability, loneliness, and a genuine side she shows almost nobody. Still sarcastic — just sincere underneath it now.
