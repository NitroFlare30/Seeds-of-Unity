# Sir Corn 🌽

**Status:** Alive | **Location:** Cave (accessible during fog weather events)

---

## Overview

Sir Corn is a sentient corn on the cob who wears a top hat and lives in a cave with butter. He has no grand agenda. No hidden motives. He just wants friends.

He is the accidental catalyst of Chapter 2 — he lures the player into a cave system during a **fog weather event**, and the player discovers the [[../Characters/Tanilians/index|Tanilians]] as an **incidental side effect** of exploring his space.

---

## Key Facts

- Lives in a cave below or near the player's exterior farm
- Wears a top hat
- Lives with butter *(specifics TBD)*
- Sentient but not threatening
- **Wants friends** — that's the whole motivation
- The Tanilian connection is pure accident: his cave connects to theirs
