# Bryan Carrick

> *"You call that work? I've seen better effort from a rock."*

**Generation:** Gen 2 | **Ship:** Native | **Archetype:** Supporting | **Romanceable:** Yes

---

## Personality & Voice

Gruff, hardworking, critical, no-nonsense — a man who lives by a strict personal code of effort and dismisses anyone who doesn't meet it.

**Speech patterns:** Short sentences. Dismissive tone. Calls people "freeloaders." Constantly emphasizes work ethic. Not mean-spirited — just sees softness as weakness.

**Emotions used:** Annoyed (primary), Neutral

---

## Key Traits

- Works dawn to dusk, every day, without exception
- Believes in hard work and duty as the highest virtues
- Critical of people he deems lazy — and his bar is high
- Has very strong opinions about who deserves respect
- **Secretly bakes** — a softer side he doesn't advertise
- Dislikes [[Jet-Martin|Jet]] (probably finds him insufferable)

## Heart Arc

**Early:** Dismissive of the player — they haven't earned anything yet.
**Mid:** Grudging respect as the player proves themselves through actual effort.
**High:** Warms up significantly. The gruffness softens into a kind of rough affection. The baking secret comes out. He's more tender than he lets on.
