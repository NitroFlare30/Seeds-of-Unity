# Darryl Everett — "Uncle Dink"

**Generation:** Gen 1 | **Ship:** Native | **Status:** Alive but presumed dead

---

## Overview

Darryl is one of the settlement's most tragic figures — alive but unreachable. He survived the [[../World/Settlement-History#the-atkins-incident|Atkins Incident]] but was mentally broken by what he witnessed. He wandered outside the bubble and was presumed dead. He lives near the [[../World/Outside-the-Bubble#tanilian-village|Tanilian village]], running a junk shop, talking to the binary stars. **[[../Farming/Himmies|Himmies]] keep him company.** He has no memory of his past.

---

## Key Facts

- Born of **Matthew Atkins'** secret affair with **Jaycee Willow** — **Atkins' biological son**, not Marco Everett's
- This secret died with both Atkins and Jaycee; Darryl cannot access it
- Half-brother to [[Alex|Alex Everett]] through Jaycee — neither knows
- Was married to **Celia** (Catia Ziegler's daughter) — she believes she is widowed
- **Eugene Clemens** was adopted by Celia after Darryl's disappearance
- Celia will eventually **discover Darryl is alive** outside the bubble, but he won't recognize her

---

## Outside the Bubble

Darryl's junk shop is a quiet, strange place. He talks to the stars. Himmies visit him — perhaps the ecosystem recognizes something in him that he's lost access to himself. He is peaceful, not suffering. Just... gone from who he was.

---

## The Secret

The affair and Darryl's true parentage is one of the settlement's buried secrets. Both parties who knew are dead. Darryl's lost memory makes it inaccessible from his end. It is effectively gone — unless someone pieces it together from scraps.
