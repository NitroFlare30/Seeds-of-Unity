# Alex Everett

> *"Patience, my young friend. The fish always comes to those who wait."*

**Generation:** Gen 1 | **Ship:** Native | **Archetype:** Secondary

---

## Personality & Voice

Wise, reflective, grandfatherly, philosophical. Alex speaks with warmth and earned perspective — a man who has lived through loss and found peace on the other side of it.

**Speech patterns:** Uses "hohoho" as a laugh. Calls people "my young friend." Speaks about patience, wisdom, and the passage of time. Warm and encouraging without being saccharine.

**Emotions used:** Happy, Neutral, XD (rarely annoyed)

---

## Key Traits

- Lost his wife **Corinne** and their child in childbirth complications
- Sees the player as a surrogate son/family figure
- Loves fishing and cooking — particularly Corinne's burrito recipe, which he still makes
- Found peace through spirituality and a sacred book
- Mentor figure — deeply kind and supportive, never preachy
- Shares his backstory only at high heart levels, when trust is earned

## Family Notes

Alex's parents are **Jaycee Willow** (Serenity crew) and **Marco Everett** (Tranquility crew). His wife Corinne was one of Miku Ishikawa's surrogate daughters, meaning she had deep ties to the settlement's founding generation. Her death — along with their child — is the wound Alex carries most quietly.

His half-brother **Darryl** (Atkins' secret biological son through Jaycee) is alive outside the bubble but has no memory of his past. The secret died with Atkins and Jaycee.

---

## Heart Arc

**Early:** Warm from the start — friendly, philosophical, fatherly. Easy to talk to.
**Mid:** Opens up about Corinne and the fishing spot. Starts treating the player as someone special.
**High:** Shares the full story of his losses, his sacred book, and what he found on the other side of grief. The player becomes family.
