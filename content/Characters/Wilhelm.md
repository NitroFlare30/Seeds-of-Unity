# Wilhelm

**Generation:** Gen 1 | **Ship:** Native | **Archetype:** Background

---

## Personality & Voice

Practical, stern, careful mentor — Wilhelm leads with craft, not conversation.

**Speech patterns:** Reserved and direct. Few words; those words count. Safety-conscious. Evaluates people by their work.

**Emotions used:** Happy, Neutral

---

## Key Traits

- Blacksmith and shop owner — runs the most skilled craft operation in the settlement
- Adoptive guardian of [[Lena-Miller|Lena]] (along with Amber)
- Mentor to [[Nikki-Taylor|Nikki]] — teaching carefully, not rushing her
- Practical and safety-conscious — won't let Nikki do hard work until she's ready
- Skilled craftsman who values quality above speed
- Protective of his apprentices and family
