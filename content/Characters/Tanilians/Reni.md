# Reni

**Species:** Tanilian | **Role:** Child (Outgoing) | **Romanceable:** No

---

## Overview

Young girl. The bold one. **The first Tanilian to approach the player** — walks up and asks them for food because the box Atkins used to fill has been empty for a long time and she's hungry. Energetic, talks freely once comfortable. Brave out of necessity, not just personality — she's had to be.

---

## Key Traits

- Initiates first contact with the player; the adults hang back
- Her hunger is the catalyst that moves the relationship forward
- Energetic and talkative once she decides someone is okay
- **Believes her parents are gone temporarily and will come back** — nobody has told her the truth
- Brave in the way children are when they don't fully understand the danger

---

## The Parents

Reni's parents **did not make it onto the escape ship**. They sacrificed themselves so she and [[Mevi|Mevi]] could escape. Every adult in the group knows. Nobody has told the children — they're protecting the innocence while they can.

Reni believes they'll return. The weight of that sits with the adults around her.
