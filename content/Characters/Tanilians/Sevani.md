# Sevani

**Species:** Tanilian | **Role:** Skeptic | **Romanceable:** No

---

## Overview

Woman. Reserved and guarded. Not confrontational — just **quietly, consistently closed off**. Her distrust of humans isn't rooted in one specific event; she simply refuses to ever be in a position where she could be enslaved again. Trusting any outsider is a risk she won't take.

---

## Key Traits

- Not hostile — just unwilling to open a door that could close on her again
- Her caution is rational, not irrational — she's protecting herself and her people
- The player has to earn her trust **slowly and deliberately over a long period of time**
- May never fully trust — and that's valid
- Unlikely to move into the settlement even if the border opens; too close to feeling contained again

---

## Notes

Sevani's arc is about the limits of trust — the story doesn't demand she change. It asks whether the player can make space for someone who won't easily open up, and whether that's enough.
