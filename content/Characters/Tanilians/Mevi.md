# Mevi

**Species:** Tanilian | **Role:** Child (Quiet) | **Romanceable:** No

---

## Overview

Young boy. [[Reni|Reni]]'s companion, similar age (couple years difference). **Extremely quiet** — not mute, just rarely speaks. When he does say something, it carries weight. Hangs back while Reni approaches. More cautious. May suspect more about his parents than Reni does.

---

## Key Traits

- Doesn't initiate; observes first
- When he speaks, it means something
- More cautious and internally aware than Reni
- May intuit that his parents aren't coming back, even without being told — the difference between Reni's hope and Mevi's quiet is the gap

---

## The Parents

Same as Reni — their parents didn't make it. Mevi and Reni's parents sacrificed themselves. The adults protect their innocence. But Mevi may already sense it.

Moving into the settlement at the end of the game would carry **extra weight** for Mevi and Reni — they have no parents anchoring them outside.
