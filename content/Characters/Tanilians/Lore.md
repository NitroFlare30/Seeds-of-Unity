# Tanilian Lore

---

## Species Overview

Tanilians are **turtle-humanoid aliens** — refugees from a planet where they were enslaved by a tortoise-humanoid species called the **Tortilians**. They escaped to Raea by hijacking a ship already destined for the planet.

All Tanilians speak English. The reason is unknown and treated as coincidence for now.

---

## Biology

- Age very slowly — **lifespan 200+ years**
- Childhood is proportionally much longer than humans
- Reproduce slowly — low population growth rate
- Age visibly like real tortoises — ancient elders look ancient (Master Oogway reference point)
- Think on completely different timescales than humans

---

## Their Escape

A group of surviving adults, plus **Reni and Mevi** — two children whose parents sacrificed themselves so the children could escape. The parents were almost certainly killed by the Tortilians after the ship left. No adult in the group has told the children. Everyone is protecting their innocence. Reni and Mevi believe their parents are just gone temporarily and will return.

The escape was enabled by **a tortoise soldier** known only by his designation number — never a name. He was motivated by loneliness and disillusionment with his own people. He fed the Tanilians the information they needed. He almost certainly was tortured to death after the ship left.

**Elaren carries this.** He remembers the number, not a name, because that's all there was. This detail reflects how the Tortilian empire dehumanizes even its own.

---

## The Tortilians

- Heavy, bureaucratic, domineering culture
- Soldiers and workers have **designation numbers, not names** — intentional dehumanization
- Will **not pursue the Tanilians to Raea** — too remote, not worth the commitment for a handful of refugees
- Exist as background lore and the reason the Tanilians can never go home

## Naming Conventions

### Tanilian Sound Palette
- **Consonants:** l, n, m, v, r, t, d, k
- **Vowels:** a, i, e *(avoid heavy o/u — reserved for oppressor names)*
- **Structure:** 1–2 syllables for young/common; 3 syllables for elders/formal
- **Feel:** Flowing, soft, trailing endings — avoid hard stops

**Examples:** Kaevi, Dalen, Mireth, Tivak, Narel, Selin, Vaken, Dekari, Elaren, Taleni, Nalemi, Reni, Mevi, Daven, Sevani, Linera

### Oppressor (Tortoise) Sound Palette
- **Consonants:** g, k, r, th, v, b, gr, kr
- **Vowels:** o, u, au, or
- **Structure:** 2–3 syllables, often end in hard stop
- **Feel:** Heavy, bureaucratic, domineering

**Examples:** Goruth, Vakorn, Throdu, Korvel, Drauvik

**Soldiers/workers have designations, not names** (e.g. "254321") — intentional dehumanization.
