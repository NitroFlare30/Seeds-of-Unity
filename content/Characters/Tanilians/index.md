# 🐢 Tanilian Characters

Tanilians are **turtle-humanoid aliens** and refugees from their home planet. They were enslaved by a tortoise-humanoid species called the **Tortilians** and escaped to Raea by hijacking a ship already destined for the planet.

They live in a small village outside the settlement bubble, beneath and around the player's exterior farm. All Tanilians speak English — reason unknown, treated as coincidence for now.

## Biology

- Age very slowly — **lifespan 200+ years**
- Childhood is proportionally much longer than humans
- Reproduce slowly — low population growth rate
- Age visibly like real tortoises — ancient elders look ancient
- Think on completely different timescales than humans

---

## Their History

A group of adults who survived slavery, plus **two children (Reni and Mevi)** whose parents sacrificed themselves to get them on the escape ship. The children were prioritized specifically to save them from being put to work.

Their escape was made possible by a **tortoise soldier** — known only by his designation number, never given a name — who fed them the information they needed. He almost certainly was tortured to death after the ship left. Elaren remembers the number. Not a name. Because that's all there was.

**The Tortilians will not pursue them to Raea** — too remote, not worth an interstellar expedition for a handful of refugees. They exist as background lore and the reason the Tanilians can never go home.

---

## Group Dynamics

- No formal governance structure — slavery never allowed them to develop one
- Everyone defers to **Elaren** — not because he has a title, but because he got them out
- Adults are guarded and carry generational trauma
- **Children are more open** — first to warm to the player, which slowly softens the adults
- **Daven** bridges the gap earliest — purely practical, works alongside the player before full trust
- **Sevani** is the last to trust — possibly never fully
