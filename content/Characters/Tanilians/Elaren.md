# Elaren

> *"I know what happened with your farmer. I don't blame him."*

**Species:** Tanilian | **Age:** 150+ | **Role:** Elder | **Romanceable:** No

---

## Overview

Very old, even by Tanilian standards. Gentle, quiet, patient. Speaks slowly. Doesn't need to raise his voice. **Tired in a deep way** — not defeated, just worn. The one who planned and executed the escape. Has no formal title — he's just the person everyone defers to because he got them out.

---

## What He Carries

- Grief over the **nameless soldier** (designation number only) who died for their escape
- Guilt over the **parents** who didn't make it to the ship
- The weight of being the closest thing **Reni and Mevi** have to a grandfather
- Awareness of what **Mayor Atkins** tried to do — and grace about why it failed

---

## Key Traits

- Cautious about trusting humans — he knows individuals can surprise you (the soldier proved that), but he's not willing to bet his people's lives on it easily
- Warms to the player **slowly and deliberately**
- **Knows who Mayor Johnson is** — has watched the settlement from outside for years; Johnson has never bothered to learn about Elaren
- Not a formal leader, but everyone defers to him

---

## Story Role — Chapter 3 Climax

The final negotiation scene mirrors Elaren and Mayor Johnson as two authorities carrying the weight of past failures:

- Johnson walks in **defensive**, carrying the deaths from the Atkins incident
- Elaren walks in **without resentment** — he understands the difference between malice and ignorance
- **The turning point:** Elaren says something quiet like *"I know what happened with your farmer. I don't blame him."*
- Johnson wasn't expecting grace from the person with the most reason to be bitter. **This cracks him open.**

---

## Heart Arc

**Early:** Distant, polite, watching. The player must earn his attention.
**Mid:** Begins to engage — curious about the player's motives, asks careful questions.
**High:** Shares the story of the nameless soldier. One of the most emotionally heavy moments in the game.
