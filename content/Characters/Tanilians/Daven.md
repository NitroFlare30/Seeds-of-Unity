# Daven

**Species:** Tanilian | **Role:** Pragmatist | **Romanceable:** No

---

## Overview

Young man, equivalent to mid-twenties. Ambitious, purpose-hungry — his first real taste of freedom and he doesn't know what to do with it yet. The pragmatist: doesn't care about politics or trust or history, just wants to **learn to farm**. Latches onto the player naturally because the player accidentally gives him direction and purpose.

---

## Key Traits

- No formal role in the group — nobody has formal roles. He just stepped toward the work.
- The first Tanilian to work **alongside** the player before full trust is established
- Bridges the gap between the player and the group earliest — purely practical
- Ambitious in a way that's just starting to find its shape
- The freedom from slavery is new; he's still learning what to want

---

## Story Role — Chapter 3 Climax

In the final negotiation scene, Daven is one of the two **advocates** in the room, mirroring [[../Lucas-Walker|Lucas]]:

- **Lucas:** diplomatic, frames everything as economic benefit, speaks Mayor Johnson's language
- **Daven:** direct, purpose-hungry, speaks from lived Tanilian experience
- Both are young, both proving something — together they cover both sides of the table
