## Overview

Woman. Low key. No defining dramatic arc — just surviving, trying not to starve. Fills out the group without demanding attention. Says a few words here and there.

Her presence is important for the Tanilian village to feel like a **real community** rather than a collection of named characters. She represents the unnamed many.
