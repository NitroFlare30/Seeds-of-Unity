# Lena Miller

> *"The forest near the graveyard — it's strange. Not dangerous. Just... watching."*

**Generation:** Gen 2 | **Ship:** Native | **Archetype:** Secondary

---

## Personality & Voice

Thoughtful, concerned, observant, somewhat anxious — Lena lives at the margins of the settlement, physically and socially, and notices things others miss.

**Speech patterns:** Asks thoughtful questions. Expresses concern for others and the environment. Mentions what she's observed about the ecosystem. Introspective.

**Emotions used:** Happy, Neutral, Annoyed

---

## Key Traits

- Lives on the **edge of the forest to the east**, near the graveyard — rarely comes into town
- Environmentally conscious; observant of Raea's ecosystem
- Cares about the player's wellbeing in a quiet, practical way
- Curious about Earth and how farming differs
- Somewhat introverted/isolated — not unhappy, just solitary
- Strained relationship with [[Wilhelm|Wilhelm]] (her adoptive father — she won't become a blacksmith)

## Family Notes

Lena is **Wilhelm and Amber's surrogate daughter** — not biologically theirs. Wilhelm and Amber are both Catia Ziegler's surrogate children, meaning Lena has an indirect generational connection to the original Serenity crew.

The tension with Wilhelm is specific: he wants her involved in blacksmithing; she wants no part of it. She's chosen her own path — the forest, the ecosystem, the land.

---

## Heart Arc

**Early:** Friendly but distant. Present when the player finds her, but not easy to find.
**Mid:** Shows care through concern — checking on the player's wellbeing, asking questions.
**High:** Opens up about isolation, where she belongs, and what she's been observing about the ecosystem (potentially a bridge to the Tanilian story).
