# Emily Scott

## Personality & Voice

Fun, sarcastic, prankster energy — bored with life on Raea and making her own entertainment at everyone else's expense.

**Speech patterns:** Casual and teasing. Uses "xd" frequently. Jokes constantly. Teases relentlessly. Calls things boring. Calls people stinky. Not malicious — genuinely just having fun.

**Emotions used:** Happy, XD, Neutral, Annoyed

## Key Traits

- Pulls pranks constantly — puts fish in the Mayor's office, sets snares in trees
- Particularly targets [[Jet-Martin|Jet]] and [[Bella-Johnson|Bella]] for maximum drama

## Family Notes

Emily was born as one of **Ariana's surrogate children** and was raised by **Ben** and his wife **Hanna**. Hanna died when Emily was young — Ben has been quietly depressed ever since and actively avoids discussing Hanna with Emily. **Ariana** gives Emily small snippets about Hanna in quiet defiance of Ben's wishes.

Emily doesn't fully know why Ben is the way he is. Uncovering Hanna's story is part of her high-heart arc.

---

## Heart Arc

**Early:** Pranks the player, teases relentlessly, treats everything as a joke.
**Mid:** Starts showing the player is worth keeping around (by not pranking them as hard). Warms up.
**High:** Genuine vulnerability. The boredom is a mask. She's been carrying loneliness about Hanna she doesn't fully understand. Becomes real, sincere, and still funny.
