# Julian Garcia

> *"The ocean doesn't need eyes to be beautiful. Neither does the music."*

**Generation:** Gen 2 | **Ship:** Native | **Archetype:** Supporting | **Romanceable:** Yes

## Personality & Voice

Poetic, reflective, peaceful, cultured — Julian experiences life richly through every sense but sight, and wouldn't trade his perspective for anyone else's.

**Speech patterns:** Eloquent and thoughtful. Uses flowery language. Loves poetry and nature descriptions — particularly through sound, smell, texture. Deeply appreciative of beauty he finds in non-visual ways.

**Emotions used:** Happy, XD

## Key Traits

- **Blind** — uses non-visual senses to build a rich inner world
- From a ranching family (the Garcias) but not interested in ranching
- Loves literature and books; visits the library frequently
- Appreciates and enjoys [[Arno-Nkosi|Arno]]'s music — they have a natural kinship
- Intellectual and cultured; thinks deeply about everything
- Grateful for his upbringing despite it being far from his nature
