# Jesse Wilson

> *"Welcome to the ranch! Don't worry, Spuddy the potato doesn't bite."*

**Generation:** Gen 2 | **Ship:** Native | **Archetype:** Supporting | **Romanceable:** Yes

---

## Personality & Voice

Friendly, hardworking, humorous, self-aware — Jesse loves farming unironically and makes no apologies for it.

**Speech patterns:** Casual and warm. Makes puns about farming. Uses capitalization for emphasis. Names his crops. Quotes farmer wisdom. Gets frustrated about ruined work clothes.

**Emotions used:** Happy, XD, Annoyed, Neutral

---

## Key Traits

- Runs a small ranch on Raea
- **Names his crops** — Spuddy the potato is canon
- Passionate about ranching life; takes it seriously but not solemnly
- Friendly and willing to teach newcomers the ropes
- Gets genuinely frustrated when his work clothes get ruined
- Ranch hand alongside [[Darryl-Everett|Damien Garcia (current)]]
