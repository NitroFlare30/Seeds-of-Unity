# Lucas Walker

> *"There's something off about the records. It's probably nothing. ...It's probably not nothing."*

**Generation:** Solace crew | **Ship:** Solace | **Archetype:** Main

---

## Personality & Voice

Positive, knowledgeable, supportive, diplomatic — Lucas is the kind of person who finds the path forward even in difficult terrain.

**Speech patterns:** Encouraging tone. Mentions settlement history he's been absorbing rapidly. Appreciative of others' work. Optimistic about the future without being naive.

**Emotions used:** Happy, XD, Neutral

---

## Key Traits

- **Best friends with Matthew Atkins** on Earth during training; designated as Atkins' vice mayor
- Arrived on Solace expecting to reunite with Atkins; found a grave instead. **Carries quiet guilt he wasn't there.**
- Intellectual and knowledgeable — has been studying the settlement's history obsessively
- Diplomatic and entrepreneurial — he speaks the language of mutual benefit
- **Plants the seed of Chapter 1's conspiracy**: notices an artificially curated gap in settlement records and mentions it casually to the player
- Loyal to the player since childhood (on Earth, before the Solace mission)
- In Chapter 2: proposes the trade agreement with the Tanilians, framing it in economic terms Mayor Johnson understands

## Story Role

Lucas is the **diplomatic engine of the story**. He's the one who:
1. Plants suspicion about hidden information (Chapter 1)
2. Brokers the peace between the player/Tanilians and Mayor Johnson (Chapter 2)
3. Stands in the room at the final negotiation as one of the two advocates alongside Daven

He speaks Mayor Johnson's language — economic benefit, practical outcomes, proof — and uses it to get the right result.

---

## Heart Arc

**Early:** Encouraging and curious. Shares what he's learned about the settlement. Positive presence.
**Mid:** Shares the grief about Atkins quietly. His optimism has a wound in it.
**High:** Becomes fully personal. The Atkins guilt, the gap in the records, what he hoped for and what he found. Romantic and sincere.
