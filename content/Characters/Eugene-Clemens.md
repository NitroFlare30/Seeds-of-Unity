# Eugene Clemens

> *"Oh! I'm so sorry, I forgot — wait, no, I didn't forget, I just — I'm sorry."*

**Generation:** Gen 1 | **Ship:** Native | **Archetype:** Supporting | **Romanceable:** Yes

---

## Personality & Voice

Anxious, helpful, flustered, nervous — Eugene is perpetually one small thing away from a minor crisis, and he apologizes for all of it.

**Speech patterns:** Apologetic tone as default. Overthinks out loud. Stutters when flustered. Eager to help. Worries easily. Says sorry whether he did anything wrong or not.

**Emotions used:** Happy, Neutral

---

## Key Traits

- Works as [[Alfred-Johnson|Mayor Johnson]]'s secretary/assistant
- Anxious and people-pleasing — hates conflict
- Very forgetful, but genuinely helpful when he gets it together
- Embarrassed easily
- **Knows private things about the Mayor** — occupational hazard
- Constantly apologizing, but not a pushover at high hearts

## Family Notes

Eugene was **adopted by Celia** after her husband Darryl disappeared outside the bubble. Celia believed herself widowed and took Eugene in to cope with the loss. He was raised as her son.

His adoptive father Darryl is alive outside the bubble — but has no memories. Neither Celia nor Eugene knows this.

---

## Heart Arc

**Early:** Flustered by the player's attention, apologizes constantly, helps in small anxious ways.
**Mid:** Gets more comfortable. Still nervous but more genuinely himself.
**High:** Reveals he's more capable and perceptive than he seems. The anxiety is real but so is the competence underneath it. Shares what he knows about the Mayor — carefully.
