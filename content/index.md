# 🌱 Seeds of Unity

A colony life-sim RPG set on **Planet Raea**, following the descendants of humanity's first interstellar settlers. Players navigate relationships, survival, and the mysteries of their enclosed bubble settlement on an alien world.

> **Setting:** Planet Raea · Enclosed bubble settlement · ~Year 2134+ (Solace arrival)
> **Settlement founded:** ~2072 (Serenity arrival)
> **Calendar:** Raean Calendar (custom seasons)
> **Travel:** Settlement residents are restricted from leaving the bubble
