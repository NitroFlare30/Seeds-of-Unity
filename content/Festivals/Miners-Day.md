# ⛏️ Miner's Day

**When:** Mid-summer
**Where:** The Nkosi cave inside the settlement, named after Lubanzi Nkosi
**Type:** Human-only

Stays inside the bubble, so the player doesn't need access to the outside [[Mining/index|mine]].

---

## Activities

- **Honoring Lubanzi:** A short opening at the cave mouth hosted by his family: [[Characters/Luhan|Luhan]] (son) and [[Characters/Arno-Nkosi|Arno]] (grandson).
- **Gem Hunt:** A timed dig in a roped-off, pre-seeded section of the cave. The player keeps what they find, and the rarest find wins.
- **Miner's Lunch:** A big shared meal outside the cave, out of the summer heat. The main place for NPC conversations.

## Reward

Ore and minerals from the dig, plus a rare mineral for the winner. Can serve as an early material source before the outside mine opens.

## Tone

Proud, hard-working, grounded. A day about the people who built the settlement.

## Optional Lore Hook

Lubanzi discovered the cave system, and the Tanilians hide in a cave system under the player's farm. If those are the same caves, his legacy quietly connects to the main story.
