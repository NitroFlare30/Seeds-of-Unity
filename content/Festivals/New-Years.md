# 🎆 New Year's

**When:** The night the year turns (early January)
**Where:** Town square
**Type:** Human-only

The only festival that runs late at night.

---

## Activities

- **Countdown:** Everyone gathers in the square for a midnight countdown and a light show (fireworks or the bubble's lights).
- **Resolution jar:** Everyone drops a resolution into a communal jar. The player's resolution is a small yearly goal, such as "ship 100 crops," "befriend 5 people," or "catch 20 fish." At the next New Year's the jar is opened, and the player is rewarded if they met their goal.
- **NPC resolutions:** Read out when the jar is opened. Good for character beats and running jokes.

## Tone

Social, upbeat, and forward-looking. The most upbeat of the winter festivals, following [[Ember-Night|Ember Night]]'s quieter family focus.
