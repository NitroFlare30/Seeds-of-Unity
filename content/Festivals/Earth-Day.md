# 🌍 Earth Day

> [!note] Draft
> Summary drafted but not yet reviewed.

**When:** Late February, the last festival before spring
**Where:** Indoors, at the settlement's records / archive space
**Type:** Human-only

---

## Activities

- **Earth artifacts display:** Photos, tools, and keepsakes brought on the ships go on display once a year.
- **Storytelling night:** Traditionally, people who remember Earth tell stories to the Raea-born. Charlotte Wilson is the last living early-crew member and her memory is going, so the Solace arrivals (the player, [[Characters/Lucas-Walker|Lucas]], [[Characters/Jet-Martin|Jet]], [[Characters/Nikki-Taylor|Nikki]]) are now the only ones with recent memories of Earth, and everyone wants to hear from them.
- **Earth dinner:** Ariana cooks Earth recipes passed down from the original crew, some half-remembered or adapted to what grows in the bubble.

## Story Hook

Charlotte's Earth is from the 2070s; the Solace crew's is from the 2130s. When their stories don't match, the generations collide on screen, and the player gets a festival where they're the one being asked for stories.

## Tone

Nostalgic, curious, bittersweet. A quiet end to winter before [[Planting-Day|Planting Day]] starts the year over.
