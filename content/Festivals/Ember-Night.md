# 🎄 Ember Night

**When:** December, lined up with real-world Christmas
**Where:** Town square, then people's homes
**Type:** Human-only

---

## Activities

- **Lighting the tree:** The settlement has one real Earth evergreen, grown from a seed brought on the Serenity. Every Ember Night it's decorated and lit. It's the settlement's one shared tradition carried over from Earth.
- **Open gift-giving:** Gifts given that night count for more (e.g., double friendship), and NPCs give the player gifts back based on relationship level. Deliberately not a secret-santa draw.
- **Family dinners:** After the lighting, everyone goes home, and the player is invited to one household's dinner (whoever they're closest to). Plays off the settlement's surrogate and pieced-together families: Ben and [[Characters/Emily-Scott|Emily]], [[Characters/Alfred-Johnson|Mayor Johnson]] and [[Characters/Bella-Johnson|Bella]], [[Characters/Miya-Ziegler|Miya]] and Charlotte.

## Tone

Warm, quiet, homey. The smallest-feeling festival even though the whole town takes part.

## Optional Depth

Holidays are hard for the grieving characters. Celia believes she's a widow; [[Characters/Alex|Alex]] lost his wife and his mother. A line or two from them, or skipping the tree lighting, adds weight without bringing down the whole festival.
