# 🏆 Harvest Fair

**When:** Mid to late fall, when Earth crops peak
**Where:** Town square
**Type:** Human-only

The biggest and busiest human festival, and the payoff for the farming year.

---

## Activities

- **Produce judging:** The player enters their best interior-farm crops. The settlement's smaller, non-official farmers compete too: **Ariana, Ben, and 1–2 others (TBD)**.
- **Judge:** Proposed: [[Characters/Alfred-Johnson|Mayor Johnson]], since Ben can't judge while competing. *Not confirmed.*
- **Cooking contest:** Villagers enter dishes made from the harvest (if the cooking system supports it).
- **Stalls and games:** Simple carnival games and a festival-only shop.

## Reward

Ribbons or trophies that can be displayed on the farm, plus festival currency or a rare item.

## Tone

Loud, competitive, celebratory. The high point before winter slows everything down.

## Design Note

This is intentionally the one festival that feels close to Stardew Valley's fair. That's accepted.
