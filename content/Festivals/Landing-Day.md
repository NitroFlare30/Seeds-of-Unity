# 🚀 Landing Day

**When:** Early fall
**Where:** Town square, then the inn
**Type:** Human-only

The anniversary of the **Serenity** landing in 2072.

---

## Activities

- **Mayor's speech:** [[Characters/Alfred-Johnson|Mayor Johnson]] gives a short address about the founders and the settlement's survival. It sets up his "protect what we have" position before the bubble opens later in the [[Story/Chapters|story]].
- **Party at the inn:** After the speech, everyone parties at the inn. Ariana, the inn's cook, hosts.

## Tone

A founders' day: proud, with grief underneath.

## Keeping It Distinct from Earth Day

Landing Day is about **arriving on and surviving Raea**. [[Earth-Day|Earth Day]] is about **the Earth they left behind**.
