# ✨ Twin Stars

**When:** Late spring / early summer, at dusk
**Where:** A lookout or open field where both stars are visible at once
**Type:** Human-only, romance

Uses the binary stars as a symbol of pairing instead of generic hearts and flowers.

---

## Activities

- **Star-gift exchange:** Courting gifts are coded to a star: warm-toned for the red star, cool-toned for the blue star.
- **Stargazing:** The player can invite a romance interest to watch the stars together. This is the date/dance equivalent: mostly dialogue and presence, with no real minigame.

## Tone

Warm and personal. A small cast (whoever the player is courting) rather than a town-wide event.
