## Unity Festival

The first joint human–[[Characters/Tanilians/index|Tanilian]] celebration, after the trade agreement and the bubble opening. Public and communal, mixing food and traditions. The thematic capstone of the game.

## Night of Names

A Tanilian remembrance tradition honoring those who didn't make it: the nameless soldier who fed them the escape information, and [[Characters/Tanilians/Reni|Reni]] and [[Characters/Tanilians/Mevi|Mevi]]'s parents. Private by default; the player earns an invitation through deep trust. Ties into [[Characters/Tanilians/Elaren|Elaren]]'s heart-event material.
