# 🎉 Festivals

Most festivals are **human-only** settlement traditions. Only two late-game festivals involve the [[Characters/Tanilians/index|Tanilians]].

---

## Calendar Rules

- **Earth-style calendar inside the bubble.** The settlement runs on an artificial Spring / Summer / Fall / Winter calendar. Raea's [[Farming/Five-Seasons|five binary-star seasons]] only apply outside the bubble, so no human festival is tied to them.
- **Winter is intentionally busier.** Winter has three festivals because there is little farm work in the off-season.
