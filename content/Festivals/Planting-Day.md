# 🌷 Planting Day

**When:** Early spring (Spring 1 or Spring 3)
**Where:** Town square / communal plot
**Type:** Human-only

The season opener, marking the first till of the interior farm.

---

## Activities

- **Seed handouts:** Shop owners hand out free **flower seed** packets.
- **The First Seed:** A small communal ritual where one seed is planted in the square. Charlotte Wilson, the last living early-crew member, leads it. She half-remembers the real first planting 60+ years ago, and [[Characters/Miya-Ziegler|Miya]] gently steers her through it.
- **Seed Race:** A simple minigame: race to plant and water a row first. The player can join. A natural fit for Ariana's surrogate kids ([[Characters/Bella-Johnson|Bella]], [[Characters/Emily-Scott|Emily]], [[Characters/Ayla-White|Ayla]], [[Characters/Jesse-Wilson|Jesse]], [[Characters/Bryan-Carrick|Bryan]]), since it's a rare day they all show up together.

## Reward

The Seed Race winner gets a small early-game boost, such as a fertilizer stack or a rare starter seed.

## Tone

Light, hopeful, and forward-looking. Not a heavy story beat; the generational material is saved for [[Earth-Day|Earth Day]].
