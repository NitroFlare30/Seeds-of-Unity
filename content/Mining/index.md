# ⛏️ Raea Mining System

The mine is a grid-based, depth-sequential dungeon reached through the [[World/Outside-the-Bubble|Mine Entrance]]. Mined minerals feed the farming system's [[Farming/Crystal-Nodes|crystal nodes]] and unlock crystal crop access.

---

## Quick Reference

| System | Details |
|---|---|
| **Structure** | Grid-based floors, depth-sequential unlock |
| **Checkpoints** | Elevator every 5 floors |
| **Floor generation** | Procedural, except every 5th "vault" floor (hand-authored) |
| **Ore tiers** | Copper → Iron → Silver → Gold → Gems (10 types) |
| **Core puzzle** | Resonant crystal chain detonation |
| **Risk system** | Greed — escalating risk that punishes overstaying, without loot loss |

---

## What Makes Mining Distinct

1. **[[Mine-Structure|Mine Structure & Depth Tiers]]** — floor layout, checkpoints, ore/gem distribution
2. **[[Resonant-Crystals|Resonant Crystal Puzzle]]** — shape-based chain detonation and loot multiplier
3. **[[Greed|Greed Subsystem]]** — risk that escalates the longer you delay banking loot
4. **[[Hazards|Mine Hazards]]** — unstable tiles, gas pockets, corrupted/wild crystals

---

## Naming Note

"Crystal" already refers to the farming system's [[Farming/Crystal-Nodes|crystal nodes]]. The mineable puzzle rocks in this system are called **resonant crystals** throughout, to avoid collision.
